## Facial Recognition Prevention

Physical Alterations

1.

Hairstyle Changes - Switching between long & short hair, varying haircuts. Makes it harder for computers to recognize important facial landmarks.

2.

Makeup Techniques - Applying cosmetics, contrasting patterns on the face. Makes it harder for computers to match one's face to the available data.

3.

Wearable Accessories - Wide brimmed hats, large sunglasses, anti-facial recognition masks. Hide significant portions of the face without suspision.

Clothing Choices

1.

Patterned Clothing - Complex or irregular patterns worn near the face, like a high-collared jacket or a scarf. For enhanced protection, use scarfs with a CV Dazzle Camouflage. The system is looking for the usual arrangement of eyes, nose & mouth but the scarf's pattern disrupts the facial contours, disrupting facial landmark analysis.

2.

Hoodies & scarves - Pulling a hoodie tight around the face or wrapping a scarf can obscure the shape of the head & neck. Computers will not be able to determine a person's identity based on their silhouette.

Tech Solutions

1.

Infrared LED Glasses - Glasses equipped with infrared LEDs can emit light invisible to the human eye but can blind facial recognition cameras. When activated, these LEDs emit infrared light which creates blind spots on the footage captured by surveillance cameras.

2.

Reflective Materials - Clothing or accessories made with reflective materials can bounce back the light from cameras, causing glare that can obscure the face in digital images. The special reflective fabric is embedded with thousands of tiny glass beads that act like mirrors, reflecting light directly back towards its source.
