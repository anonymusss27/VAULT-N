These are evidence-based compounds that have shown anti-cancer activity, oncogenic reversal or risk reduction:
1. Broccoli sprouts/cruciferous vegetables: It has been studied most notably in prostate cancer but has also tested in breast, colon, prostate, bladder, pancreatic, and lung cancer models.

2. Turmeric: the main compound is curcumin. The clearest human testing has been colorectal adenomas. It has also been explored preclinically in breast, prostate, pancreatic, gastric, liver, and brain cancers tested in the lab.
3. Flaxseed: It has been tested most clearly in breast cancer, and more recent reviews mention activity in colon, liver, and prostate models
4. Soy foods: Soy has been studied most strongly in breast cancer, where it was associated with lower mortality and lower recurrence. Soy compounds have been also studied in prostate, colon and endometrial cancer also with promising results.
5. Green tea: It has been tested most directly in prostate cancer prevention settings. Outside prostate cancer, green tea catechins have also been studied in breast, colorectal, liver, oral, and lung cancers,
6. Tomatoes: The main compound is lycopene. Tomato and lycopene research has been tested most often in prostate cancer. Other reviews associate lycopene with lower overall cancer risk and lower cancer mortality
7. Garlic: Garlic has been tested most directly in colorectal adenomas / colorectal cancer prevention.
8. Black rasperries. They been tested in a surprisingly wide range of early clinical settings, especially oral cavity lesions/head and neck, esophageal lesions and Barrett’s esophagus, and colorectal/colon lesions and polyps.
9. Mushrooms: The main compounds depend on the species, but higher mushroom consumption is associated with lower total cancer risk, particularly associated with less breast cancer.
10. Walnuts: The most relevant human testing I found is in colon polyp / colorectal cancer prevention-related settings. Walnut-related metabolites may help create a more favorable inflammatory and immune environment.
