Things that kill confidence
1. Rehearsing conversations in your head.
2. Trying to say the “right” thing instead of the honest thing.
3. Overanalyzing how you sounded after talking.
4. Changing personality depending on who you’re with.
5. Laughing or agreeing just to fit in.
6. Apologizing when you did nothing wrong.
7. Avoiding speaking first.
8. Comparing yourself in every room.
9. Thinking everyone is judging you.
10. Forgetting people are thinking about themselves, not you.
11. Needing approval before making decisions.
12. Hiding opinions to avoid conflict.
13. Saying yes when you want to say no.
14. Trying to impress instead of connect.
15. Staying quiet to avoid embarrassment.
16. Overthinking messages before sending.
17. Assuming people dislike you.
18. Judging yourself mid-conversation.
19. Trying to act confident instead of being present.
20. Forgetting who you are when you’re around others
