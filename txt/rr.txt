Social media has RUINED the expectations of a man in a relationship...

We are letting fake internet standards ruin perfectly good relationships, and it is getting so toxic.

1. The “If he wanted to, he would” trend has been completely weaponised
Originally, this quote meant that if a guy wanted to text you back, make time to see you, or treat you with respect, he would. But social media twisted it. Now, girls are using it to say, “If he wanted to, he would buy me a Cartier bracelet and take me to Dubai.” Babe, no. Stop using a quote about emotional effort to justify demanding expensive materialistic things.

2. A 23-year-old lad is just trying to build his life
Let’s be so real for a second. A guy in his early twenties is literally just starting his career, trying to pay his rent, and figuring his own life out. He is grafting. Expecting a 23-year-old to be buying you designer bags, expensive clothes, and paying for random holidays is pure delusion. That is not normal, and putting that kind of financial pressure on a young guy is incredibly unfair.

3. He is your partner, not your personal ATM
You are supposed to be his teammate, not a financial burden. If you want a brand new Prada bag or a luxury holiday, go to work and buy it for yourself! A relationship is meant to be a 50/50 partnership where you build an empire together. Expecting him to just hand over his hard-earned money to fund your aesthetic lifestyle is a massive red flag on your part, not his.

4. True love is literally priceless
We are losing the plot on what actually matters. A healthy relationship isn’t measured by how much money he drops on you. It’s measured by how safe he makes you feel, how he looks after you when you’re sick, and the fact that he’s fiercely loyal. The best things in a real relationship—peace of mind, genuine laughs, and unwavering support—literally do not cost a single penny.
