1️⃣ White tongue

🧠 Your tongue is the mirror of your gut.

⚠️ A white-coated tongue usually means candida overgrowth, low stomach acid, or poor oral microbiome.

✅ Start oil pulling with coconut oil every morning. Add small fermented foods like sauerkraut and kefir. Ditch the processed carbs.

👅 Tongue scraping is your new non-negotiable.

2️⃣ Bloating after eating

🧠 Bloating is not normal. It’s not ‘just your body’

⚠️ That’s a sign of poor digestion, bacterial overgrowth, or food intolerances.

✅ Slow down. No phone, no stress. Chew your food 20–30 times. Ditch seed oils, ultra-processed foods, and eat mindfully

🥩 Bone broth, ginger, and bitters before meals can be game changers.

3️⃣ Cold hands and feet

🧠 Warmth is circulation. Circulation is vitality.

⚠️ Chronic cold extremities? Could mean poor thyroid function, weak metabolism, or low iron.

✅ Eat more red meat, organ meats, and ditch raw salads for cooked, warming foods. Prioritise iodine, selenium, and B vitamins.

🔥 Layer up, move more, and stop skipping breakfast.

4️⃣ Waking up between 2–3AM

🧠 When your liver or cortisol wake you up, your habits need fixing.

⚠️ Waking at this hour regularly? Could be blood sugar crashes, liver congestion, or cortisol spikes.

✅ Cut off caffeine by 1pm. Avoid blue light. Eat a protein-fat snack before bed (like Greek yoghurt + honey or eggs).

🌙 Support your liver with lemon water and bitter greens.

5️⃣ Strong body odour

🧠 If your smell A LOT, your detox pathways are clogged.

⚠️ You’re not dirty… you’re likely toxic. Liver, lymph, or gut dysfunction can cause a shift in your body’s natural scent.

✅ Ditch chemical deodorants. Use natural products like magnesium-based deodorants. Sweat daily. Dry brush. Sauna if possible.

🌿 Smell is a symptom, not the root.

6️⃣ White spots on nails

🧠 Your nails are nutrient report cards.

⚠️ These spots often scream zinc deficiency. But can also mean poor absorption due to gut issues.

✅ Eat oyster and beef liver. Heal the gut with colostrum, fermented foods, and minerals.

💅 Your nails will thank you in a few weeks.

💥 You’re just disconnected from your body’s signals.
